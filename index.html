About Valmoria

Valmoria is a small kingdom ruled by King Thomory, with its capital in Berain. The king holds real power, and citizens vote on the laws.

The flag

Three horizontal stripes carry a gold crown inside a ring of twelve gold stars.

  • Red for courage
  • Green for ground
  • White for peace
  • Gold for the crown and the stars

How Valmoria is governed

Valmoria is a semi-constitutional monarchy. Citizens vote on laws. The king may veto any law they pass, and he has the final word.

The Stars in Valmoria

The national anthem.

Verse 1

Twelve stars stand above the crown,
silent over field and stone.
We who keep this ground and town
keep it as our own.

Chorus

The horizon of home is honored here.
Let it be kept, and let it be known.
Beneath the stars we stand together,
and we will not leave it alone.

Verse 2

The crown is old, the road is long,
the people bear it well.
Let the quiet be our song
and our word be kept as well.
